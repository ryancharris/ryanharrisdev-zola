I've been writing software for over a decade, and I still think the best part of the job is making other engineers' lives less painful. These days I do that at dbt Labs, where I work on distributed systems and internal tooling — the kind of stuff that doesn't ship features but makes the people who do ship them a lot faster.

My career started in Philly, bouncing through Revzilla, Guru, and Fauna, where I did everything from building UIs to leading full app re-platforming efforts. Somewhere in there I made some video courses on egghead.io and gave a few conference talks, which was terrifying and fun in equal measure.

This blog is basically my thinking out loud in public — code, tools, whatever problem has taken over my brain that week. No editorial calendar, no content strategy, just whatever I find interesting.
